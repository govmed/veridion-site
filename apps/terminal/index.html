SSH Terminal & SFTP

A real shell, and your files, from your pocket

iPhoneiPadMacAndroid

A native, privacy-first SSH client that does two things well: a genuine interactive terminal, and SFTP file transfer over the very same connection. Everything runs from your device straight to the machines you configure.

A real interactive shell

PTY-backed, xterm-256color, full colour, live resize. vim, htop and tab completion behave the way they do on a desktop.

Hardware keyboard support

Arrows, Ctrl-C and the rest go straight through to the remote shell rather than being eaten by the app.

SFTP on the same connection

Browse the remote filesystem, upload from Files and download to your device, with live progress and cancellable transfers.

Keys sealed behind Face ID

OpenSSH Ed25519 and RSA keys, or passwords — stored in the Secure Enclave and unlocked biometrically. The whole app can be locked too.

Host keys pinned on first contact

Trust-on-first-use verification, so you know the server answering is really yours.

Nothing collected

No accounts, no sign-up, and no servers operated by the app.
