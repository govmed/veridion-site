Moderno Terminal — Privacy Policy

Moderno Terminal — SSH & SFTP is an SSH client and SFTP file manager for Android, published by Veridion LLC. It does not collect your data. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, and no server operated by us. The same policy covers the app on Apple platforms, where it is published as SSH Terminal & SFTP.

Last updated: 23 August 2026

ON YOUR DEVICE

What the app stores

To do its job the app saves the following on your device only: the connection profiles you enter (hostname, port, username, and a display name), your preferences, and the host-key fingerprints it pins the first time you trust a server. These live in the app's own private storage. Deleting the app deletes them.

KEYS AND PASSWORDS

Sealed by the operating system

If you choose to save a password or an SSH private key, it is encrypted with a hardware-backed key in the Android Keystore and released only after your fingerprint, face, or device credential unlocks it. It is used solely to authenticate to the servers you name, and is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can remove it at any time by forgetting the host or deleting the app.

NOT COLLECTED

What the app doesn't do

No accounts and no sign-up. No analytics, no telemetry, and no crash-reporting service. No advertising, and the app does not request or use an advertising ID. No third-party tracking SDKs. The app makes no network requests of its own — the only connections it opens are the SSH connections you ask for.

YOUR CONNECTIONS

We are never in the path

The app connects directly from your device to the SSH and SFTP servers you configure. The commands you run and the files you move travel between your device and those servers over an encrypted SSH connection. We have no access to that traffic and no record that it happened. The servers themselves are operated by you or by third parties, under their own policies rather than this one.

FILE TRANSFERS

You choose every file

Uploads and downloads go through Android's own file picker. The app receives access to exactly the file or folder you select, for that transfer, and it reads no other storage on your device.

PURCHASES

Moderno Terminal Pro

The free tier saves up to three hosts and includes the whole terminal. A single one-time purchase lifts that limit and unlocks the SFTP file manager, sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment and tells the app only whether the purchase is owned. The app never sees your name, your payment details, or your Google Account. Google's handling of that transaction is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

PERMISSIONS

Why each one is requested

Internet and network state to reach the servers you configure. Biometric to unlock saved credentials. Foreground service so a running session is not killed while the app is in the background. Notifications to show that a session is still open. The app requests no location, contacts, camera, microphone, or broad storage access.

Children: the app is a developer utility, is not directed at children, and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes: if this policy changes, the updated version is published here and the date above changes.
