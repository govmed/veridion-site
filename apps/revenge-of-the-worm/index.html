Revenge of the Worm

The worm is done being prey

iPhoneiPadMac
Games · Free · iOS 18 · iPadOS 18 · macOS · rated 9+
View on the App StorePrivacySupport

The classic snake arcade reimagined as a living 3D terrarium: a smoked-glass board, insects that scurry, march in columns and sometimes explode, and one very hungry worm with a score to settle.

Eat, grow, molt

Hunt ticks, termites and beetles, each worth different points. Hit maximum length and molt — cash the tail in for points and extra lives.

Things that hunt back

A wasp dives for your tail; a boss erupts through the glass. Health, hearts and armour decide whether a wall is fatal.

A pack economy worth chasing

Eleven packs across four rarities — shields, armour, pulse, lasers, thorns, hearts, wall passes. Legendary drops split the worm in two and let the tail hunt alone.

Built for competition

Top-ten leaderboards per difficulty with arcade-style initials, and three difficulties that change bombs, acid, bosses and speed.

Collects no data

No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no network features at all.
