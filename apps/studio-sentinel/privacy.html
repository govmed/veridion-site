Studio Sentinel — Privacy Policy

Studio Sentinel has no backend. It talks only to the agent running on your own local network, and nothing it reads reaches the internet.

Policy last updated 13 May 2026 · republished under Veridion LLC on 1 September 2026. The terms are unchanged; only the publishing entity's name has.

Data collection

Studio Sentinel does not collect, transmit or store any personal data on external servers. The app has no backend, uses no analytics services, and does not track users.

Local network only

The app communicates exclusively with the Studio Sentinel Agent running on your local network. All metrics — CPU, memory, thermal and process — stay within that network and are never sent to the internet.

What is stored on your device

A pairing token in the iOS Keychain to authenticate with your agent; agent IP addresses and ports in UserDefaults for reconnection; alert rules and preferences in UserDefaults. None of it leaves your device.

In-app purchases

Studio Sentinel offers an optional Pro upgrade through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Transactions are handled entirely by Apple; we neither receive nor store any payment information.

Third-party services

The app integrates no third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks or crash-reporting services.

Children's privacy

Studio Sentinel is a developer tool and is not directed at children.
