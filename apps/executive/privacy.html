Executive — Privacy Policy

Executive keeps your data on your device. There is no account, no Veridion server holding your material, and no analytics or tracking of any kind.

Effective 7 September 2026.

What the app holds

Approvals, commitments, parked ideas, voice memos and their transcripts, calendar context, and business metrics. Confidential free text — transcripts, commitment wording, parked notes, approval details — is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM. The key is generated on the device, stored in the iOS Keychain, and never leaves it.

What it reads, only if you allow it

Calendar, to show today's schedule. Contacts, to address a drafted message. Microphone, to record voice memos. Speech recognition, to transcribe them on the device. Each is requested when you first use the related feature, each can be refused, and the app works with all of them denied. Calendar events and contacts are read when needed and are not saved into the app's own storage.

You decide, per source

Beyond the system permissions, every source — calendar, approvals, metrics, voice memos, commitments, contacts, parked items — can be set to Off, On device, or Device + Model in Settings, and changed at any time.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you enable it, your data replicates through your iCloud account's private database so it appears on your other devices. That is Apple infrastructure tied to your Apple ID, and Veridion LLC has no access to it. Disabling sync keeps everything local.

Your organisation's metrics feed (optional)

If you connect a metrics feed, the app requests figures from the address your organisation provides, authenticating with a token you enter. The request only fetches — no personal data, memo content or usage information is sent.

An AI model (optional, off by default)

Executive analyses voice memos on your device by default, using Apple's own frameworks. You may instead route a transcript to a model provider, which requires both that source set to "Device + Model" and a credential you supply. Withhold either and nothing is transmitted. When enabled, the transcript is sent to the provider you configured and is subject to their terms. The app records that a transfer happened — the time, the source, the destination category — but never the content.

What we never do

We never send your data to ourselves; there is no Executive server holding your content. We never sell or share it. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash reporter and no tracking. And the app never sends a message on your behalf — it prepares a draft and hands it to Messages or Mail, where you decide.

Your activity record

The app keeps a tamper-evident log of its own significant actions — a consent change, an export, a transfer to a model, a screenshot. Entries record what kind of event occurred, when, and which item it concerned, identified internally. They never contain your content and never a person's name. You can read it in Settings → Activity record.

Deleting your data

Delete individual items at any time. Deleting the app removes its local data. If iCloud sync was enabled, remove the app's data in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Executive also protects itself if the device falls into someone else's hands: after five consecutive failed authentication attempts it destroys its encryption key and deletes its local data. This is irreversible and cannot be undone by us.

Retention

The app can be configured to discard each kind of content after a period you choose. By default nothing is discarded automatically; you remain in control of what is kept. Security records are retained regardless, because they are the entries an investigation would need.

Children

Executive is a professional tool intended for adults and is not directed at children.
