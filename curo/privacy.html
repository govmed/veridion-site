Curo — Privacy Policy

Curo is a focus timer for macOS and iOS, published by Asarel Morales. It does not collect your data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

Last updated: 20 August 2026

ON YOUR DEVICE

What Curo stores

Everything Curo records stays on your device: your focus sessions (the intention you typed, how long you planned, how long you actually focused, and when), the thoughts you park and whether you have dealt with them, and your preferences. These are ordinary files in the app's own storage. They are never transmitted anywhere, and deleting the app deletes them.

NOT COLLECTED

What Curo doesn't do

No analytics and no crash-reporting services. No advertising and no advertising identifiers. No third-party SDKs. No network requests of its own — Curo has no reason to contact a server, and does not.

PURCHASES

Curo Pro

Sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the payment and tells Curo only whether a subscription is currently active. Curo never sees your name, payment details, or Apple Account. Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

NOTIFICATIONS

If you allow them

Curo uses notifications to tell you a session's planned time is up, or to check in mid-session. They are generated on your device. Nothing is sent through a server.

Children: Curo is not directed at children, and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes: if this policy changes, the updated version is published here and the date above changes.

Contact: questions about this policy — support@veridion-llc.com.

Curo is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, or manage any condition, including ADHD, and nothing in the app is medical advice.
