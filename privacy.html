Revenge of the Worm — Privacy Policy

Effective August 5, 2026

Revenge of the Worm collects no data.

The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no network features. Your scores, preferences, and progress are stored only on your device, and are never transmitted to us or anyone else. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it.
