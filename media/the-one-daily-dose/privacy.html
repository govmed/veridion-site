The One Daily Dose — Privacy Policy

The One Daily Dose is a TikTok app registered by Veridion LLC to read the statistics of its own TikTok account, @the.one.daily.dose, into Veridion's private dashboard. It has no public users.

Effective 4 October 2026.

What the app reads

Through TikTok's Display API, and only for a TikTok account that has signed in and consented, the app reads the account's public profile figures (display name, follower count, following count, likes, video count) and the public statistics of that account's own videos (title, description, posting time, share link, views, likes, comments, shares). The only account that consents is Veridion's own.

What it never does

It never posts, edits, deletes or labels anything. It does not request any upload or publish permission, and it does not read private messages, direct messages, drafts or data of any other account.

Where the data goes

The figures are stored on Veridion's own computer and shown on Veridion's internal dashboard. They are not sold, shared, or transferred to any third party, and they are not used for advertising. Access tokens issued by TikTok are stored on that same computer, readable only by the owner, and are used solely to repeat the reads above about once a day.

Cookies and tracking

This page sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The app itself has no website or form that collects information from anyone.

Your choices

A TikTok account that has consented can withdraw access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions. Veridion then deletes the stored token.
