The One Daily Dose — Terms of Service

The One Daily Dose is an internal tool of Veridion LLC. It exists to read the statistics of Veridion's own TikTok account into Veridion's private dashboard, and it is not offered to the public.

Effective 4 October 2026.

1. Who may use the app

The app is operated by Veridion LLC for its own TikTok account and for test accounts Veridion itself adds while testing. No other person or business is granted access, and nothing on this page is an offer of a service to the public.

2. What the app does

With the consent of the signed-in TikTok account, the app reads that account's public profile figures and the public statistics of its own videos through TikTok's Display API, and shows them on Veridion's dashboard. It does not post, edit, delete or label content. How the figures are handled is described in the privacy policy.

3. TikTok's own terms

Use of the app is subject to TikTok's Terms of Service and TikTok for Developers' terms, which take precedence over this page wherever they apply.

4. No warranty

The app is provided as is, for Veridion's internal use, without warranty of any kind. Veridion may change, suspend or retire it at any time without notice.
