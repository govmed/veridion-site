About the shows

One inbox: support@veridion-llc.com. A subject line from below gets you routed fastest.

Corrections

Accuracy is the whole product. If any fact, number or attribution in an episode is wrong, email with subject “Correction”, the episode, and a link to your source. Verified errors are corrected and acknowledged — that is a house rule, not a favour.

Rights & takedown

All presenters are original AI-generated characters — no real person's voice or likeness is cloned. If you believe content affects your rights, email with subject “Rights” and we will respond promptly.

Press & partnerships

Interviews, media enquiries, or working together — subject “Press”. Press bios and our transparency standards are published on this site and free to quote.

Everything else

Questions about the shows, the anchors, or how AI-generated news media can be trustworthy — we actually like that question. Subject “Hello” works fine.

What support can't do: give financial advice, discuss individual investments, or recommend anything with a ticker on it. The dogs don't, the desk doesn't, and the inbox doesn't either.
